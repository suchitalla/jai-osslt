Ontario Literacy — OSSLT Practice Test

Original, unofficial practice · 35 selected-response questions + 2 writing tasks · Two sessions

Instructions

Work through the reading and writing questions. Select the requested answer(s), then complete the two written tasks. Your answers are saved in this browser. Submit when finished to see the answer key and explanations.

Session A: information paragraph, news report and writing questions 1–6, plus a short written response. Session B: dialogue, real-life narrative and writing questions 7–12, plus an opinion essay.

This practice uses an original question set and is not affiliated with EQAO. Automated scoring applies only to the 35 selected-response questions; writing requires human review. There is no official pass/fail prediction.
